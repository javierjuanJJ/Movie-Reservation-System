# Roadmap (Order of Features)

1. 001-user-auth: Sign up, log in, roles (Admin + User), Better-Auth seed
2. 002-movie-management: CRUD movies (Admin only)
3. 003-showtime-management: Schedule shows and halls
4. 004-reservation-management: Seat selection, reserve, cancel (User), transactional integrity
5. 005-reporting: Admin endpoint - all reservations, capacity, revenue
6. 006-payment-processing: Stripe integration for seat charging
7. 007-email-notifications: Resend email on reservation
8. 008-sms-notifications: Twilio SMS confirmation
